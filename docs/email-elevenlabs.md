**Asunto:** Data processing details for a healthcare deployment (EU)

Hello,

We run a voice receptionist for medical and dental clinics in Spain and
Portugal, built on ElevenLabs Agents. Our customers are the data
controllers, we are their processor, and ElevenLabs is our sub-processor.
Because the calls contain patients' health information, our customers'
data processing agreements require us to document your part precisely.

Three questions, and we would be grateful for answers in writing:

1. **Where are audio recordings and conversation transcripts stored?**
   Specifically the region or regions, and whether any processing or
   storage happens outside the EEA. If data does leave the EEA, which
   transfer mechanism applies.

2. **Which of your own sub-processors touch that data?** We are asking
   about anything that handles call audio or transcripts: infrastructure,
   speech recognition, and the model providers behind the agent's LLM.
   A current list, or a link to one you keep updated, is what we need.

3. **Do you offer a standard data processing agreement we can sign?**
   If so, please send it. If it is only available on certain plans, tell
   us which, because that changes what we can offer our customers.

For context on what we have already configured: retention is set to seven
days with deletion of both audio and transcripts, and we understand that
conversation history redaction requires an enterprise subscription and is
therefore not available to us. If there is another way to reduce what is
retained on your side for a healthcare workload, we would like to hear it.

Thank you,

Bweb Studio
